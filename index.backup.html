ABOUT GREAT DORMASS

A proud tradition of knowledge, skill and character.

Dormaa Senior High School, popularly known as DORMASS, began as Dormaa State College on 24 February 1947. The school was founded through the work of Joseph Manase Oppong and Samuel Kwadwo Yeboah.

The school’s published vision is to be the pacesetters for academic standards, growth and development, with a mission focused on equipping young people with knowledge, skills and the right attitude.
